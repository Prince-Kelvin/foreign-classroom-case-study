# Stakeholder & Persona Analysis

## 1. Purpose

Stakeholder analysis was used to understand the different users and business participants interacting with the Foreign Classroom platform, their objectives, responsibilities, pain points, and information needs.

The analysis helped ensure that requirements were considered from both the end-user and operational perspectives.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Overview

| Stakeholder | Role | Primary Objective | Key Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student | Primary platform user | Find suitable international study opportunities and apply | Simple search, accurate programme information, application visibility |
| Influencer | Student acquisition partner | Refer prospective students and monitor applications | Application visibility, commission information, profile management |
| Super Agent | Recruitment partner | Manage and support student applications | Student/application visibility, commission tracking, transaction history |
| Processing Staff | Operations user | Process and manage student applications | Application queue, student information, processing status |
| Finance Staff | Finance operations | Manage commissions and payment-related activities | Transaction records, commission information, withdrawal processing |
| Administrator | Platform administrator | Manage users, assignments, configurations and operations | Administrative controls, reassignment, visibility and oversight |

## 3. Persona 1 — Student

### Profile

The student is the primary customer of the Foreign Classroom platform and uses the system to explore international education opportunities.

### Goals

- Find suitable schools and programmes.
- Filter opportunities using relevant criteria.
- Compare available options during decision-making.
- Review programme information.
- Submit applications.
- Track application progress.

### Pain Points

- Difficulty discovering suitable programmes.
- Too many search options without effective filtering.
- Inconsistent or incomplete information.
- Uncertainty about application progress.
- Repeated communication with staff for basic status information.

### Key Requirements

- Flexible programme and school search.
- Country and city filtering.
- Programme-level and programme-type filtering.
- School and faculty/department filtering.
- Intake-year filtering.
- Clear search results.
- Access to programme details before application.
- Application status visibility.

---

## 4. Persona 2 — Influencer

### Profile

The Influencer supports student acquisition by referring prospective students to the platform.

### Goals

- Refer prospective students.
- Monitor applications generated through referrals.
- Understand potential commission.
- Maintain accurate profile information.

### Pain Points

- Limited visibility into referred applications.
- Difficulty tracking application progress.
- Unclear commission information.

### Key Requirements

- Application dashboard.
- Application status visibility.
- Student and programme information.
- Potential commission information.
- Profile management.
- Promotional/referral information.

---

## 5. Persona 3 — Super Agent

### Profile

The Super Agent manages student recruitment activities and may work with associated subagents.

### Goals

- Manage student applications.
- Monitor recruitment activity.
- Track commissions.
- Review transaction history.
- Manage withdrawal requests.

### Pain Points

- Fragmented application information.
- Difficulty tracking commission activity.
- Limited visibility into financial transactions.

### Key Requirements

- Application visibility.
- Student information.
- Commission tracking.
- Transaction history.
- Withdrawal management.
- Relevant filtering and reporting.

---

## 6. Persona 4 — Processing Staff

### Profile

Processing Staff are operational users responsible for managing and progressing student applications.

### Goals

- Receive assigned applications.
- Review student information.
- Process applications efficiently.
- Maintain accurate application statuses.
- Communicate with students when required.

### Pain Points

- Manual application handling.
- Unclear ownership of applications.
- Difficulty identifying pending work.
- Delayed reassignment when staff are unavailable.

### Key Requirements

- Application dashboard.
- Assigned application visibility.
- Student information.
- Application status management.
- Clear ownership/assignment.
- Administrative reassignment support.

---

## 7. Persona 5 — Finance Staff

### Profile

Finance Staff manage financial activities relating to agents, influencers and other participating partners.

### Goals

- Review commission information.
- Monitor payments and withdrawals.
- Maintain transaction records.
- Update payment-related information.

### Pain Points

- Difficulty maintaining a consolidated view of transactions.
- Limited visibility across different partner categories.
- Manual tracking of commission updates.

### Key Requirements

- Finance dashboard.
- Agent and Influencer financial information.
- Partnered School financial information where applicable.
- Commission information.
- Transaction history.
- Withdrawal/payment management.
- Notes and audit visibility for commission updates.

---

## 8. Persona 6 — Administrator

### Profile

The Administrator is responsible for platform-level configuration, oversight and operational intervention.

### Goals

- Maintain platform configuration.
- Manage users and assignments.
- Monitor operational activities.
- Resolve exceptions.
- Ensure appropriate staff ownership of student interactions.

### Pain Points

- Manual intervention when staff are unavailable.
- Difficulty monitoring ownership of operational activities.
- Need for appropriate access controls.

### Key Requirements

- User management.
- Role-based access.
- Staff assignment and reassignment.
- Operational oversight.
- Exception handling.
- Administrative controls.

## 10. Stakeholder Requirement Prioritisation

Requirements were prioritised by considering:

1. Impact on the student journey.
2. Operational importance.
3. Financial or business impact.
4. Frequency of use.
5. Risk of process failure.
6. Dependency on other platform functions.
7. Impact on user experience.

This approach helped distinguish essential platform capabilities from supporting features.

---

## 11. BA Insight

The stakeholder analysis demonstrated that the platform could not be designed solely around the student experience.

Although the student is the primary customer, successful delivery also depends on the operational ecosystem behind the application journey.

For example:

**Student searches → Student applies → Processing Staff process application → Finance manages relevant financial activity → Agents/Influencers monitor their involvement → Administrator provides oversight and intervention.**

This wider perspective helped connect customer requirements with operational and business requirements.
